How fast do I get the system?

48–72 hours after we have your intake answers. Done-with-you: kickoff within 24h of payment; system within 48–72h after kickoff.

What do you need from me?

A short intake (~15 minutes): niche, offer, audience, 3–5 links or posts you like. Voice notes optional.

Which platforms?

Copy is LinkedIn / X / Instagram-ready. You pick a primary channel; we adapt tone per platform.

Can I revise?

Starter: none included. Standard & Done-with-you: 1 revision round. DWY also gets 7-day async tweaks via Slack or email.

What’s the refund policy?

If we don’t deliver a usable 14-day system in 72 hours (clock starts when intake is complete), full refund. No refund after delivery acceptance for “I changed my mind.”

Do you post for me?

No. This is an install, not a retainership. You get the system + SOP. Posting is yours (or your VA’s).

What tools do you use?

Google Docs / Drive by default. Notion ops board on Standard and Done-with-you.

Who is this for / not for?

For founders, coaches, and agencies who already know what they sell but post inconsistently. Not for people who want ongoing posting management, brands with no offer yet, or “make me viral” requests.
